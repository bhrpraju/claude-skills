05 Design Principles
⚖️
RASI over RACI
"Consulted" is dropped. "Supports" replaces it. Every action has exactly one Responsible owner — no shared accountability ambiguity.
🔺
Trichotomy: Why → What → How
Every action captures intent before task. No jumping to tasks without understanding the objective driving them.
🚫
No speech-based reporting
Never "Raj said X." Always subject-based: "The team confirmed that..." Names only for ownership and escalation.
📍
Negative decisions logged
Decisions NOT to act are explicitly captured. "The team decided not to proceed until client approval is received" — this is a decision.
🚩
Missing info protocol
Nothing invented. Owner unclear / Not stated — every gap is visible, never papered over.
🔗
Holarchy check
Every major item connects upward: meeting → project → business → org outcome. Unclear linkage is flagged, not assumed.
